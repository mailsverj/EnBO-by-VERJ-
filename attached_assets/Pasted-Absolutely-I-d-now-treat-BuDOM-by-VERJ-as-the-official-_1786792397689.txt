8. BATTERY SIZING

The separate Backup Hours field is removed.

BuDOM calculates night-time energy from the appliance schedule.

Formula

Battery Required (kWh) = Total Night-time Load Energy × 1.25

The 1.25 factor represents the specified allowance for:

* 20% DOD allowance
* 5% inefficiency

The system should calculate the night-time energy automatically from the individual appliance entries.

⸻

9. BATTERY SELECTION

After calculating required storage, BuDOM selects the smallest available battery configuration that meets or exceeds the requirement.

Required Storage	Battery
0–5 kWh	5 kWh
5.1–10 kWh	10 kWh
10.1–16 kWh	16 kWh
16.1–32 kWh	32 kWh
32.1–48 kWh	48 kWh
Above 48 kWh	Continue progression

Multiple modules can be combined where necessary.

Battery modules should appear in designs using standardized descriptions such as:

16kWh 51.2V Lithium Battery Module × 2

⸻

10. PV SIZING

BuDOM uses:

PV Required = (Total Battery Size ÷ 6 hours + Total Load) × 1.67

The system must clearly distinguish:

* Calculated PV requirement
* Selected panel wattage
* Number of panels
* Total installed PV capacity

⸻

11. INVERTER SELECTION

The design engine should:

1. Determine inverter requirement from total load.
2. Check PV requirement.
3. Determine whether the next inverter class is required.
4. Search inventory for an appropriate inverter.
5. Select the nearest available acceptable size.

The maximum permitted deviation from the intended inverter size is:

5 kW

If no suitable inverter exists within that tolerance:

DESIGN REVIEW REQUIRED

The system must not silently select an unsuitable inverter.

The progression should be scalable rather than hard-coded only to today’s inverter sizes.
