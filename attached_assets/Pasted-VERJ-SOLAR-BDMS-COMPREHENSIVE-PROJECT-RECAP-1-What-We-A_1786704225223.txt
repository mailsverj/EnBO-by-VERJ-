VERJ SOLAR BDMS — COMPREHENSIVE PROJECT RECAP

1. What We Are Building

BDMS — Business Development Management System is intended to become VERJ SOLAR’s central digital platform for managing the entire journey from:

BDO Recruitment → Training → Certification → Lead Generation → Customer Management → Technical Design → Approval → Invoicing → Sales → Commission → Financial Analysis

It should not simply be a database of BDOs.

It should become an integrated business-development, sales, technical, invoicing and management system for VERJ SOLAR.

The guiding principle is:

Every opportunity should be traceable from the person who generated it to the final project, invoice, revenue, profit and commission.

⸻

2. THE BDO MODEL

VERJ BDOs are flexible business developers.

They do not necessarily need to work full-time.

A BDO can sell VERJ SOLAR while:

* Working a 9–5
* Running a personal business
* Studying
* Working another professional occupation
* Managing other activities

Their primary responsibility is to generate business opportunities.

Important correction

BDOs do not receive leads from VERJ.

Instead:

BDOs generate their own leads.

They use:

* Personal networks
* Workplace contacts
* Family/friends
* Businesses
* Social media
* Community networks
* Referrals
* Professional connections

They then register those leads in BDMS.

⸻

3. BDO APPLICATION & RECRUITMENT

The first part of BDMS manages the BDO recruitment process.

5. KYC SYSTEM

The applicant submits:

* Selfie
* Government ID/passport

These are not automatically considered valid simply because an image was uploaded.

VERJ Admin manually checks them.

The purpose is to ensure that applicants don’t manipulate the process by uploading meaningless or inappropriate images.

⸻

6. KYC RESUBMISSION

We later agreed that BDMS should support KYC resubmission.

If the submitted ID/selfie is unacceptable:

Admin → Request KYC Resubmission

Admin should be able to provide a reason.

For example:

“Please upload a clearer image of your government-issued ID. The photograph and identification number are not sufficiently visible.”

The applicant can then resubmit the required document.

The system should ideally maintain:

* Original submission
* Resubmission
* Reason
* Date/time
* Admin responsible

The applicant should only be able to replace the requested KYC information rather than modify the entire application.

9. ASSESSMENT

The applicant completes the Google assessment after studying the workbook.

The assessment tests the required knowledge across the training material.

The Google Sheet has already been configured so that:

* Passing candidates can receive a VBDO ID
* Failed candidates do not receive an ID

The VBDO ID format is:

VBDO-0001

The numerical portion is four digits.

Examples:

VBDO-0001
VBDO-0025
VBDO-0147

Administrators must be able to search for BDOs using their VBDO ID.

VBDO-0047 — BDO Activity & Lead Register

It should record the BDO’s:

* Lead date
* Project type
* Lead status
* Project value

Activities

* Calls
* Meetings
* Follow-ups
* Customer responses
* Load information
* Quote activity

Audit information

* Who made the update
* Date
* Time
* What was updated

The BDO should not manually maintain this spreadsheet.

BDMS should automatically push relevant information into it.

13. WHY THE BACKUP MATTERS

Zoho/BDMS remains the primary operational system.

Google Sheets becomes the independent backup/archive layer.

Therefore, if:

* A Zoho page fails
* A form breaks
* A record is accidentally deleted
* A technical problem affects the application

VERJ still has a separate record of its business-development data.

BDMS should support multiple authorised VERJ users.

This is important because the system isn’t only for Admin.

Different users may include:

Full visibility.

Engineering/Technical Officers

48. The BDMS Vision

The ultimate architecture is:

BDO → Lead → Customer → Engineering → Design → Approval → Invoice → Payment → Installation → Commission → Profit

with every stage connected, searchable, permission-controlled, auditable and backed up.

Zoho/BDMS becomes the operational brain.

Google Forms/Sheets continue to support the assessment and provide independent backup infrastructure.

Engineering gets the tools to turn BDO-generated opportunities into technically sound solutions.

Finance gets visibility into invoices, revenue, costs and profitability.

Management gets a single view of the entire business-development operation.

And BDOs get a structured platform through which they can generate opportunities, track their work and ultimately earn from the business they create.
